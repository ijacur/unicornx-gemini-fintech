## ⚡️ EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Unicorn Health is building the **"PillPack + GoodRx of Central Asia"**. We solve Uzbekistan’s two most painful healthcare bottlenecks through a unified, zero-inventory digital ecosystem:
1. **DoriQuti.uz:** A monthly subscription delivery of pre-sorted, daily-scheduled medication pouches for chronic patients (Hypertension, Diabetes, Cardiac).
2. **ArzonLab.uz:** A diagnostic marketplace that fills idle capacity in private clinics, offering patients 25–35% discounts on MRI, CT, and blood tests.

---

## 🛑 THE PROBLEM
* **Chronic Disease Epidemic:** 60%+ of deaths in Uzbekistan stem from cardiovascular disease and diabetes. 8.2M+ chronic patients take 4–8 pills daily, constantly missing doses or mixing up medications.
* **Clinic Inefficiency:** 8,700+ private clinics are opening across Uzbekistan, yet MRI and CT machines sit idle 40–50% of the day during off-peak hours.
* **Fragmented Out-of-Pocket Spend:** Families spend \$2.4B annually out of pocket with zero price transparency, no subscription convenience, and chaotic pharmacy visits.
